SYNTHETIC — FOR DEMONSTRATION

AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO CREDIT AGREEMENT

dated as of April 1, 2026

between COASTAL MARINE, INC., as Borrower, and BANK OF STOIC, as Lender.

§1 Effective Date and Scope.
This Amendment No. 2 is effective as of April 1, 2026 and amends the Credit Agreement dated as of August 1, 2024 (the "Credit Agreement").

§2 Definitions.
Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Amendment No. 2 have the meanings given to them in the Credit Agreement.

§3 Amendment to Section 7.11.
(a) Section 7.11(b) of the Credit Agreement is amended so that, in addition to any add-back already permitted there, the Borrower may add back fuel hedging mark-to-market losses, to the extent deducted in determining net income, in an aggregate amount not to exceed $800,000 for any test period.

§4 Continuing Effect.
Except as expressly amended by this Amendment No. 2, every term of the Credit Agreement remains in full force and effect.

Executed by the Borrower and the Lender.
