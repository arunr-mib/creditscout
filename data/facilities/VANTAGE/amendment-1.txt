AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO CREDIT AGREEMENT

dated as of April 6, 2026

between VANTAGE PRINT WORKS, INC., as Borrower, and FAIRHAVEN COMMERCIAL BANK, as Lender.

§1 Effective Date and Scope.
This Amendment No. 1 is effective as of April 6, 2026 and amends the Credit Agreement dated as of January 27, 2025 (the "Credit Agreement").

§2 Definitions.
Capitalized terms used and not defined in this Amendment No. 1 have the meanings given to them in the Credit Agreement.

§3 Amendment to Section 9.01.
(a) Section 9.01 of the Credit Agreement is amended so that notices to the Borrower shall be sent to the attention of the Chief Financial Officer at finance@vantageprint.example. This change is administrative only.

§4 Continuing Effect.
Except as expressly amended by this Amendment No. 1, every term of the Credit Agreement, including Section 7.11, remains in full force and effect.

Executed by the Borrower and the Lender.
